Quick tech tips

Thirty-second videos on the things neighbors ask me about most. Watch one, try it, and text me if you get stuck.

Printer says offline?

Restart the printer, then the computer, and make sure both are on the same Wi-Fi. That clears most offline errors.

Aim your security camera right

Mount it 8 to 10 feet up, angle it down, and keep it out of direct sun so the picture stays clear.

Set screen time limits

Set the limits on the kid's device itself, not just by asking nicely. Then the rules hold even when you're not around.

Use a guest Wi-Fi network

Put guests and smart gadgets on a guest network, away from your computers and phones.

Slow computer? Restart it

Restart, then turn off the startup apps you never use. Most slow computers just need that.

Fake support pop-ups

A pop-up with a phone number to call is never real Microsoft or Apple support. Close it and don't call.

Change your router defaults

Swap the admin password and the Wi-Fi name that came on the sticker, so strangers can't guess their way in.

A cable beats Wi-Fi for TV

Buffering? Plug the streaming box into the router with a cable, or move it closer to the router.

Pick one smart home system

Choose Alexa, Google or Apple Home before you buy more gadgets, so everything works together.

Wire up for gaming

A cable between your console and the router means less lag than Wi-Fi.

Phone storage full?

Move photos and videos to the cloud and delete big apps you never use.

Back up your computer too

Keep one copy at home and one off-site or in the cloud. Two copies, two places.

Skip banking on public Wi-Fi

Turn on your phone's hotspot and bank on that instead of coffee shop Wi-Fi.

Wipe before you donate

Sign out of your accounts and factory reset the phone or laptop before it leaves your hands.
