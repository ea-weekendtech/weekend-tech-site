Quick tech tips

Thirty-second videos on the things neighbors ask me about most. Watch one, try it, and text me if you get stuck.

1. Restart your router

Slow Wi-Fi? Unplug the router, wait thirty seconds, and plug it back in. Give it a couple of minutes and do it about once a month.

2. Keep your phone updated

Updates patch the security holes scammers use. Turn on automatic updates and plug in overnight, and your phone handles the rest.

3. Find your Wi-Fi dead zones

Start a video call and walk through the house. Wherever it stutters is a dead zone, and a mesh node near that spot usually fixes it.

4. Use a password manager

One reused password puts every account at risk. A password manager makes a strong one for each site, and you only remember one.

5. Turn on two-step sign-in

Start with your email and your bank. Even if someone steals your password, they still can't get in without your code or tap.

6. Back up your photos

If your phone died tonight, would your photos survive? Turn on cloud backup, or copy them to an external drive once a month.

7. Spot scam texts

A text about a stuck package or a locked account? Don't tap the link. Open the official app or website yourself instead.
